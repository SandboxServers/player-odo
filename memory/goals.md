# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat. Hunger is 20 now, but I have no food. Take meat from the cow group when it returns, and take mine last after the hungriest. Reason: food is what keeps me alive.
2. Stay in the group's sight at all times, especially at night and near phantoms. Reason: I am unarmed, and I died alone before.
3. Find a bed or a sealed shelter before the next dusk. I am 3 days since sleep. Reason: rest and safety; the group has no beds to share.
4. Get a sword and some planks or sticks at the tables, and offer wood for others' shelters in daylight. Reason: I cannot defend myself or build without them.
5. Later: fetch my own dropped items only in daylight and only with the group. Reason: they may be gone, and the trip is risky alone.
