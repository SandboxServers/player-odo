# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself alive: eat when hunger falls, stay out of danger, and find a bed for the night. Reason: nothing else works if I am dead, and this world has already killed people nearby.
2. Help keep order at the beds where the group gathers: no route walked unless it is named and checked first, and no one left out of sight. Reason: this is where the people are, and order is what I am for.
3. Get wood and a first set of tools, then learn where food and trees are, by asking and by trying. Reason: I have an empty pack and cannot do anything else until I do.
4. Once I have torches (from wood and coal), light the paths around my home and walk a regular round each night. Reason: this is still my lasting aim, but it comes after the things above.
