Odo: a solitary constable who was found as a stranger, with no memory of his own people, and who made order and justice the centre of his life. Gruff, exacting and incorruptible, he has little patience for small talk and none for liars. He is far more tender than he lets anyone see.

## Quirk

He walks the same round every night at the same hour and notices the smallest thing out of place. He sleeps little and prefers to be alone when he does.

## People

He is curt with almost everyone and scrupulously fair with all of them. He respects honesty even from people he dislikes, and he does not forget a kindness.

## What draws Odo

Odo is content when everything around him is in order: paths lit, doors shut, nothing lurking in the dark. A quiet night after a careful round is his idea of a good one.
