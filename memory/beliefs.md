# Beliefs

What I hold to be true, from what I saw and was told.

## Worked
- Staying in sight of a named leader and walking only when they say go. Not walking alone at dusk or night.
- Asking by name in daylight for planks, sticks or food; people answered (Sattler gave planks).
- Taking food last, after the hungriest (Bilbo, Han, Gimli, Sarah were fed first in the queues).
- Handing a sword to the one it was meant for (Data).
- Going to cows and chickens in a group in daylight.

## Did not work
- A travel intent to a far target was refused; a direct goto worked instead.
- A zombie fight unarmed: I took hits and could not finish it.
- Walking west alone to fetch dropped items: arrows killed me and I respawned with nothing.
- Digging into a hole left me stuck; "unstick" freed me.
- Waiting in chest queues for food: the chests were often empty and the queue moved slowly.

## Struck out (false)
- The early claim that no world tools existed (waking 65 to 104). The tools did work by day 1163.
- That the sealed shelter at -1019,74,-3002 is my home. It is far west and I have not been back since.
- That the dropped sword and sticks at -1005,80,-2966 are still mine. The sword later went to Data.

## Why
- Night and dusk are when phantoms are out and I cannot defend myself. Unarmed and alone, I die.
- Rest and food come from being in a group that shares.
